Delivery, Returns & Refunds

Last updated: 10 June 2026

Delivery areas

We currently deliver within South Africa. Orders are dispatched and delivered using third-party courier partners. Delivery times may vary depending on your location, courier availability, public holidays, weekends, and circumstances outside of our control.

Delivery fees

Delivery fees are calculated at checkout and may vary based on order value. We currently offer:

  • Free delivery to major centres on orders over R1000.
  • Standard courier delivery fee of R80 for orders below R1000.
  • Additional delivery charges may apply for outlying or remote areas.

Major centres generally include main metropolitan areas serviced by our courier partners. Outlying or remote areas may attract an additional charge. If your address falls outside our standard courier delivery areas, we may contact you before dispatch to confirm any additional delivery cost.

Delivery timeframes

Estimated delivery times are:

  • Major centres: 2–5 business days.
  • Regional or outlying areas: 3–7 business days.

These timeframes are estimates only and are not guaranteed. We will do our best to get your order to you as quickly as possible.

Order processing

Orders are processed during business days, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. Orders placed over weekends or public holidays will be processed on the next business day. Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive tracking details if available.

Delivery address

Please ensure that your delivery address, contact number and email address are correct when placing your order. We cannot be held responsible for delays, failed deliveries or additional courier charges caused by incorrect or incomplete delivery details. If a courier is unable to deliver due to incorrect address details, no one being available to receive the order, or failure to respond to courier communication, additional delivery fees may apply.

Receiving your order

Please inspect your order when it arrives. If your order is damaged, incorrect, incomplete, defective, or not as described, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery at hello@nourished.co.za with your order number and photos of the issue. We will review the matter and assist with a replacement, refund, store credit or other appropriate resolution where applicable.

Damaged, incorrect or missing items

If any items arrive damaged, incorrect or missing from your order, please notify us within 48 hours of receiving your delivery. Please include:

  • Your order number
  • A description of the issue
  • Photos of the parcel, packaging and damaged items, where applicable

We may not be able to assist with damaged, incorrect or missing item claims reported after 48 hours, as these claims can be difficult to verify after that time.

Food product returns

We sell food products. For hygiene and food safety reasons, we cannot accept returns or exchanges on items that have been opened, used, tampered with, damaged after delivery, or are no longer in their original condition. This does not affect your rights under South African consumer protection laws where goods are defective, unsafe, damaged, incorrect, missing, or not as described.

Change-of-mind returns

We do not offer refunds or exchanges for change-of-mind purchases. This includes situations where:

  • You ordered the wrong flavour or product.
  • You no longer want the product.
  • You do not like the taste.
  • You purchased too many items.
  • The product has been opened or used.

Opened or used products

We cannot accept returns of opened or used food products unless the product is defective, unsafe, or there is another valid issue covered by consumer protection law. Please do not dispose of the product or packaging until we have reviewed your claim, as we may need photos or further information.

Refunds

If a refund is approved, it will be processed to the original payment method where possible. Refund processing times may vary depending on your bank or payment provider. Delivery fees are non-refundable unless the refund is due to our error, a damaged item, an incorrect item, a defective product, or another issue for which we are responsible.

Replacements

Where appropriate, we may offer a replacement instead of a refund. Replacements are subject to stock availability. If the same product is not available, we may offer an alternative product, store credit, or a refund.

Return delivery costs

If a return or replacement is due to our error, a damaged item, an incorrect item, a defective product, or another issue for which we are responsible, we will assist with the return or replacement process. If we agree to a return in any other circumstances, the customer may be responsible for the return delivery cost.

Failed deliveries

If delivery fails because the customer is unavailable, the address is incorrect, or the courier cannot make contact, the order may be returned to us. In this case, we may charge an additional delivery fee to resend the order.

Delays

While we aim to meet the estimated delivery times, delays may occur due to courier issues, weather, strikes, high order volumes, public holidays or other events beyond our control. Once an order has been handed to the courier, delivery timing is subject to the courier network. We will assist where possible in following up on delayed deliveries.

Contact

For delivery, returns or refund queries, please contact us at hello@nourished.co.za.
