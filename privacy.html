🔒 Your privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 June 2026

Nourished ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we look after it, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Information we collect

We may collect the following personal information when you use our website, place an order, subscribe, or contact us:

  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • Delivery and billing address details.
  • Order history and subscription preferences.
  • Messages you send us through our contact or wholesale forms.
  • Technical information such as your browser type, device and pages visited, collected automatically to help the site work and improve.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without the consent of a parent or guardian.

How we use your information

We use your personal information to:

  • Process, fulfil and deliver your orders and subscriptions.
  • Communicate with you about your order, delivery or queries.
  • Manage your subscription, including upcoming deliveries and changes.
  • Respond to wholesale and stockist enquiries.
  • Improve our website, products and service.
  • Send you marketing or news, where you have agreed to receive it.

Marketing communications

We will only send you marketing communications where you have opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in our emails, or by contacting us. We will continue to send essential service messages relating to your orders and subscriptions.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with trusted third parties who help us run our business, such as courier and delivery partners, payment providers, and IT or e-commerce service providers. These parties may only use your information to provide their services to us, and must keep it secure. We may also disclose information where required by law.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to keep your shopping bag working, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect how the site works.

How we protect your information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to keep your personal information secure and to prevent loss, misuse or unauthorised access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information at all times.

How long we keep your information

We keep your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, including to meet legal, accounting or reporting requirements.

Your rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or update your information.
  • Ask us to delete your information, where we are not required to keep it.
  • Object to the processing of your information in certain circumstances.
  • Withdraw your consent to marketing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your personal information, please contact us at hello@nourished.co.za.

This privacy policy is a working draft for the prototype site and should be reviewed and confirmed with a legal advisor — and your registered company details and Information Officer added — before the store goes live.
