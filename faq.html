Frequently asked questions

Everything you might want to know about our sweets, delivery and subscriptions. Still stuck? Get in touch →

What makes FUNDAY a "better-for-you" sweet?
FUNDAY Natural Sweets are made with no added sugar and no sugar alcohols, plus gut-loving prebiotic fibre and natural colours and flavours. They're designed to taste like the sweets you love, made the way they always should have been.
Is there really no added sugar?
Yes — FUNDAY contains no added sugar. The sweetness comes from natural sources rather than added cane sugar, and we don't use sugar alcohols (like maltitol or sorbitol) either. Always check the on-pack nutritional information for full details.
What are sugar alcohols, and why don't you use them?
Sugar alcohols (such as maltitol, sorbitol and xylitol) are sweeteners often used in "sugar-free" sweets. They're known to cause bloating and tummy upset for some people when eaten in larger amounts. FUNDAY leaves them out for smoother, easier snacking.
What is prebiotic fibre?
Prebiotic fibre is a plant fibre that feeds the friendly bacteria in your gut. We bake it into every FUNDAY treat so a little snack can love you back. As with any fibre, enjoy in moderation.
Are FUNDAY sweets vegan and gluten free?
Our range is made to be gluten free, and we have plant-based, vegan-certified options too — look for the Vegan tag in the shop. Always check the individual pack, as ingredients can vary by product.
Are they suitable for diabetics or specific diets?
Many people who watch their sugar intake enjoy FUNDAY, but everyone's needs are different. If you have a medical condition or specific dietary requirements, please check the on-pack information and chat to your healthcare professional first.
How does Build-a-Box work?
Pick a box size — 6, 12 or 18 bags — then mix and match your favourite flavours until your box is full. Use "Surprise me" for a ready-made mix. Head to Build your own box to start.
How does Subscribe & Save work?
Set up a monthly delivery of your favourite flavours, a custom box, or whole 12-bag packs, and save 10% on every order. You can skip, pause, swap flavours or cancel anytime. Start on the subscription page.

Delivery is a flat R80, and it's free on orders over R1000 to major centres. Most orders arrive within 2–5 business days to major centres and 3–7 business days to outlying areas. See our delivery, returns & refunds page for full details.
