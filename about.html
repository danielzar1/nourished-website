About the brand

Snacking should be fun and feel good.

At Nourished, we never believed you should have to choose between the treats you love and looking after yourself. So we went looking for sweets that deliver both — and found them.

Why we started Nourished

For too long, the sweet aisle has been a choose-your-regret zone: loads of sugar and a list of additives nobody can pronounce. We wanted something different for our families — and for South Africa.

Nourished exists to bring the world's best better-for-you confectionery to local shelves. Treats that are genuinely fun, genuinely tasty, and made with ingredients you'll feel good about sharing.

What's different

We left out the nasties

Everything you won't find in our sweets:

  • Added sugar
  • Sugar alcohols
  • Artificial colours
  • Artificial flavours
  • Gluten
  • Anything you can't pronounce

And put in the good

Our sweets are sweetened naturally and built around gut-loving prebiotic fibre — the kind of fibre your tummy's friendly bacteria love. Colour comes from real fruit and plants like beetroot, spirulina and carrot. Flavour comes from, well, actual flavour.

The result? A treat that tastes like the sweets you grew up with, made the way they always should have been.
